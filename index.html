07 / GOOD TO KNOW
NO BAD
QUESTIONS.

  
Can I book directly through this website? +
This is a demo website. The form only creates a nonbinding inquiry summary; it does not submit anything or make a reservation.

  
Are the prices on the website final? +
No. The price list is illustrative and demonstrates a possible solution. A real business would provide its own prices and terms.

  
Are the photos from a real CUTLINE studio? +
No. All photos come from Pexels and are used only as visual illustrations.

  
Can the website be adapted to a real business? +
Yes, the entire concept can be tailored to a business's name, team, services, pricing and own photography.
